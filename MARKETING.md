## Launch Offer Strategy

### Option A: The Aspirational Upgrade (Recommended)
- **Offer:** 15% off first order + free express shipping
- **Entry:** Email signup
- **Urgency:** "Launch week pricing — ends Sunday"
- **Why:** Anchors on premium positioning while rewarding early adopters

### Option B: The Risk-Reversal Launch
- **Offer:** Buy 2, Get 1 Free + 30-Day "Perfect Match" Guarantee
- **Entry:** First purchase
- **Urgency:** Limited launch inventory
- **Why:** Drives trial with volume, guarantee removes risk

### Option C: The Bundle Builder
- **Offer:** 20% off when you buy 4+ plates
- **Entry:** Cart threshold
- **Urgency:** Automatic at checkout
- **Why:** Increases AOV naturally without artificial urgency

### Recommended Launch Sequence:
1. **Week 1-2:** 15% off + free shipping (email capture)
2. **Week 3-4:** Bundle discount at 4+ units
3. **Ongoing:** Free shipping over $50, 30-day returns
